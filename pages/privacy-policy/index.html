1. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly

  • Name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address
  • Order history and product preferences
  • Account login credentials, if you create an account
  • Messages you send us through contact forms, email, or SMS
  • Reviews, photos, or other content you submit
  • Newsletter and SMS subscription preferences

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, device type, browser type and version, operating system
  • Pages viewed, time on page, referring and exit pages, click paths
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Cookie and similar identifiers (see Section 4)

Information from third parties

  • Order and payment confirmation data from our payment processors
  • Fulfillment and delivery status from shipping carriers
  • Marketplace order data where you purchase through a third-party marketplace
  • Aggregate advertising and analytics data from our ad partners
  • Publicly available social media information when you interact with our accounts

We do not knowingly collect Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, precise geolocation, biometric data, or health information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process, fulfill, and ship your orders
  • Handle returns, exchanges, and warranty claims
  • Send order confirmations, shipping notices, and service messages
  • Provide customer support
  • Send marketing emails and text messages where you have opted in
  • Personalize product recommendations and site content
  • Measure and improve site performance and product assortment
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use

We do not store full payment card numbers on our systems. Payment information is collected and processed directly by PCI-compliant third-party processors.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, pixels, tags, SDKs, and local storage to operate the Site and understand how it is used.

Categories:

  • Strictly necessary: cart, checkout, session security. Cannot be disabled.
  • Performance and analytics: aggregate usage measurement.
  • Functional: remembering preferences such as size or region.
  • Advertising and targeting: measuring campaigns and delivering relevant ads on other sites.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and through the cookie preference tool available on the Site. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent checkout from functioning.

5. Interest-Based and Internet-Based Advertising

We work with advertising partners who use cookies and similar technologies to show you Click Clocks ads on other websites, apps, and social platforms based on your activity on our Site.

Depending on your state, this activity may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, or "targeted advertising," under applicable law. You can opt out through:

  • The Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link on the Site
  • Your browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, which we honor
  • Industry opt-out tools operated by the Digital Advertising Alliance and the Network Advertising Initiative
  • Ad settings within individual platforms

Opting out does not stop ads from appearing; it stops them from being personalized based on your data.

6. Email and Text Message Communications

Email. We send transactional emails related to your orders regardless of marketing preferences. Marketing emails are sent only with your consent and include an unsubscribe link in every message.

SMS/Text. If you opt in to text messages, you consent to receive recurring automated marketing texts at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out
  • Reply HELP for assistance
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers collected for SMS with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. How We Share Information

We share personal information with:

  • Service providers listed in Section 3, under contract
  • Advertising and analytics partners, as described in Sections 4 and 5
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets
  • Legal and safety: to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, enforce agreements, or protect rights, property, and safety
  • With your direction: for example, when you share a product page or submit a review

We do not sell personal information for money.

8. Social Media and Third-Party Features

The Site may include social media buttons, embedded feeds, and share widgets. These features are operated by third parties and may collect information about your visit even if you do not click them. Your interactions with those features are governed by the operating platform's own privacy policy, not this one.

If you tag us or post publicly about Click Clocks, we may reshare that content on our own channels. You can ask us to remove reshared content by contacting us.

9. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and vendor security review.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you share information with us at your own risk. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

10. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy:

Account information While the account is active, plus a wind-down period
Marketing preferences Until you opt out, plus suppression-list retention

Analytics and advertising identifiers Per the retention window of each platform

We may retain limited information longer where required to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations.

11. Do Not Track

Web browsers may offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting. There is no common industry standard for how DNT signals should be honored, and we do not currently respond to DNT signals.

We do honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as an opt-out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising where required by law.

12. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at the address in Section 16.

Residents of certain states have additional protections for minors under 16 regarding the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

13. Your Choices

Regardless of where you live, you may:

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any message
  • Reply STOP to opt out of text messages
  • Update account information by logging in or contacting us
  • Adjust cookie preferences through the Site's preference tool
  • Request access to or deletion of your information by contacting us

14. State Privacy Rights

The rights below apply based on your state of residence. To exercise any right, use the Privacy Request form on the Site or contact us using the details in Section 16. We will verify your identity before responding, typically by confirming information already associated with your account or order.

You may use an authorized agent where permitted by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.

California

See our separate Privacy Notice for California Residents for full detail. In summary, California residents may request to know, delete, and correct personal information; opt out of sale/sharing and targeted advertising; and limit the use of sensitive personal information. California's "Shine the Light" law also allows residents to request information about disclosures to third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

Nevada

Nevada residents may submit a verified request directing us not to sell certain covered personal information. We do not currently sell covered information as defined by Nevada law, but you may still submit a request.

Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Texas, Utah, and Virginia

Residents of these states may have the right to:

  • Confirm and access whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy
  • Correct inaccuracies in your personal data
  • Delete personal data you provided or we obtained
  • Portability: receive your data in a portable, readily usable format
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, sale of personal data, and profiling in furtherance of decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects
  • Appeal a denial of a request. We will respond to appeals within the timeframe required by your state's law and explain the basis for our decision

Specific rights, response deadlines, and appeal procedures vary by state. Where your state requires opt-in consent before processing sensitive data, we will obtain it. Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, and Texas residents may exercise opt-out rights through a universal opt-out mechanism such as Global Privacy Control.

Oregon residents may additionally request a list of specific third parties to which we have disclosed personal data.
