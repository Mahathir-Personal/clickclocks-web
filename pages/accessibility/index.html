Web Accessibility Statement

Last modified: August 19, 2026

Quick Connect Footwear, LLC ("Click Clocks") is committed to making our website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we treat it as part of how we build, not a one-time audit.

Our Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

What We've Implemented

  • Keyboard navigation: all interactive elements, including the menu, carousels, filters, and forms, are reachable and operable by keyboard alone
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements
  • Descriptive alternative text on product and content images
  • Semantic structure: proper heading hierarchy, landmarks, and list markup for screen reader navigation
  • Accessible labels on all form fields, buttons, and icon-only controls
  • Color contrast meeting or exceeding WCAG AA ratios for text and interactive elements
  • Color independence: information is never conveyed by color alone
  • Reduced motion support: animations, carousels, and scroll effects are disabled when a visitor's system indicates a "prefers-reduced-motion" preference
  • Resizable text up to 200% without loss of content or function
  • Responsive layouts that work across screen sizes, zoom levels, and orientations
  • Skip navigation link to jump directly to main content

Ongoing Work

Accessibility is not finished. We currently:

  • Include accessibility review in our design and development process
  • Test with keyboard navigation and screen reader software
  • Review new pages and features before release
  • Address issues reported by visitors on a prioritized basis

We know some areas still need improvement, particularly in third-party embedded content and older assets. We are working through these.

Third-Party Content

Some parts of our site rely on third-party services: payment processing, embedded social feeds, review widgets, and shipping tools. We do not control the accessibility of that content. Where a third-party barrier prevents you from completing a task, contact us and we will help you complete it another way.

Alternative Access

If any part of our site is not accessible to you, we will provide the information or complete the transaction through another channel. Contact us by email or phone and we can:

  • Take an order over the phone
  • Provide product details, sizing, or pricing in an alternative format
  • Process returns or exchanges directly
  • Answer any question the site would otherwise answer

Feedback and Reporting a Barrier

We want to hear about problems you encounter. When you contact us, it helps to include:

  • The page URL where you encountered the issue
  • What you were trying to do
  • The assistive technology, browser, and device you were using
  • A description of the barrier
